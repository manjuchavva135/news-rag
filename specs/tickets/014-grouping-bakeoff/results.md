## Findings worth knowing

- **Grouping quality is far below the phase-1 bar** with either Qwen model (mean 0.46; 4 of 5 pages below
  0.8). This agrees with 013's dev-sample stats (mean 0.458). Changing to the other Qwen model does not fix it.
  The gap is the grouping approach/prompt (out of scope here: "one shared prompt"), or a model family not
  graded, i.e. Gemma, which this judge cannot grade.
- **Run-to-run noise:** the dry run with the same 32B setup scored 0.501 (one page 0.2 → 0.0 on the rerun).
  Judge scores on 5 pages move by about ±0.04, which is small next to the 0.16 margin.
- **`2024-12-05_BGNOP_main_01`** is `judge_skipped` (null → 1) for both: the grouping leaves no judged articles
  and the judge reports no missed regions. It inflates both means equally.
- **012 E-7 page `2024-12-06_GNOCLT_main_16`** is in the set: 32B 0.75, 30B-A3B 0.50.
- **012 NFR-2 throughput:** 5 pages at once gives 32B 0.08 pages/s and 30B-A3B 0.15 pages/s. Neither reaches 0.2,
  though 5 parallel requests understates what 16 in flight would give (the 30B-A3B MoE is about 1.8× faster).
- **Telugu reading is weak for both Qwen models** (0.34 / 0.39). A crop was checked by eye
  (`మన్మోహన్ అస్తమయం`, clean large type), and 32B answered `ప్రభుత్వ ఉపమంచు`. The scores are real.
