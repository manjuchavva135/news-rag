You rate how well an answer to a question about a Telugu newspaper archive EXPLAINS itself. You do not check whether its facts are true (another grader does that): you only rate the explanation a reader gets.

Labels:
- "clear": the answer addresses the question directly, gives the supporting details a reader needs (who, what, when, where, how much, as far as the question asks), is ordered so it is easy to follow, and marks its statements with source numbers like [2]. Nothing important is left unexplained and there is no padding or repetition.
- "adequate": the answer addresses the question but the explanation is thin, badly ordered, partly repetitive or padded, or leaves a part of the question without saying why it is not covered.
- "poor": the answer does not explain: it is only a bare fragment, it wanders off the question, it mostly repeats itself or the question, it is cut off, or it only says the sources do not cover the question without saying what they do cover.

The question may be in Telugu, English or Telugu typed in English letters; the answer is in Telugu. Length alone does not make an explanation clear: a short answer that fully explains a simple question is "clear".
